# InterviewSaathi 🚀

**InterviewSaathi** is a Vernacular Confidence Builder web application specifically designed for students transitioning from Hindi to English. It helps candidates prepare for HR and technical interviews by allowing them to speak naturally and providing real-time feedback on their use of Hindi filler words (Hinglish).

🔗 **Live Demo:** [InterviewSaathi](https://rishabh-dwivedi22.github.io/InterviewSathi/)

## ✨ Key Features
- **🎙️ Real-time Speech Recognition:** Speak your answer, and the app listens and transcribes it instantly.
- **📝 Comprehensive Question Bank:** Over 20 common interview questions carefully curated for freshers.
- **💡 Hints & Professional Templates:** Get structural hints and a professional English template for every question.
- **🔊 Text-To-Speech (TTS):** Listen to the correct pronunciation of the questions and the professional templates using the built-in browser voice.
- **🗣️ Hinglish Detection:** The app identifies common Hindi words/filler words (like *matlab, kyunki, lekin*) used during English speech and suggests the correct English alternatives.
- **📊 Confidence Scoring:** Get an instant confidence score out of 100 based on the length of your answer and your dependency on vernacular fillers.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6)
- **Styling:** Bootstrap 5, Bootstrap Icons
- **APIs Used:** Web Speech API (SpeechRecognition for listening, SpeechSynthesis for reading out text)

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Open the [live application](https://rishabh-dwivedi22.github.io/InterviewSathi/).
2. You will see an HR question on the screen. 
3. Click the **"Start Speaking"** button and answer the question naturally. 
4. If you need help structuring your answer, click **"Need Help with Structure?"** or **"Give me a Template"**.
5. Once you finish speaking, click **"Stop & Analyze"**.
6. The app will calculate your confidence score, highlight any Hindi words you used, and suggest English replacements!
7. Use the **Next** and **Previous** buttons to navigate through the questions.
